IThe House

A house built for salt, kept for silence.

Until 1962 the Mas housed the men who raked the flats by hand. We kept the lime walls, the deep sills and the long table. We added hot water and very good linen.

There is no spa, no gym and no television. There is the étang, a library of 2,400 books, two bicycles per room and an unreasonable amount of sky.
