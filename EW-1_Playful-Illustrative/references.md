## Awarded sites

### 1. Ponpon Mania
- **URL:** http://ponpon-mania.com/
- **Creators:** Justine Soulié (illustration and story) and Patrick Heng (creative development). Patrick's portfolio: https://patrickheng.com
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 22 Oct 2025 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/ponpon-mania)
- **What it is:** an interactive web comic about "a megalomaniac sheep who wants to make the world dance".
- **What to steal:**
  - The page is shaped like a panel, not a rectangle: the hero sits inside an **arched "window" cut-out**, and the foreground clouds overlap the bottom edge so the illustration bleeds out of its frame.
  - **Hand-inked black contour lines (≈3–4 px at 1×) over flat fills**, plus one light gradient for the sun glow. The look is cel animation, not vector-corporate.
  - The cast is **soft, rounded animals with exaggerated expressions**: the sheep's arms-up pose reads from 100 m away.
  - A **rotating circular text badge** ("YOUR INTERACTIVE COMIC!") works as a sticker-style secondary CTA.
  - The UI is deliberately quiet: a lowercase **Libre Franklin** nav and a black pill "read now" button. The art does the shouting and the chrome stays out of the way.

### 2. The Crumbskees
- **URL:** https://crumbskees.com/
- **Studio:** Upperquad (San Francisco): https://upperquad.com
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 17 Sep 2020 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/the-crumbskees)
- **What it is:** "a munchy, lunchy waste of time", a playable snack-monster toy site.
- **What to steal:**
  - **Concentric wobbly "blob rings"** (yellow → orange → coral → violet) radiate from the characters. It is a psychedelic tunnel built from 4–5 offset organic paths.
  - **A full-bleed marquee frame:** a looping text border ("IT'S A MUNCHY LUNCHY WASTE OF TIME", "MADE WITH <3 BY UPPERQUAD ~~~~(~@") runs vertically down both sides and across the top.
  - **An outlined, extruded black display wordmark on a yellow bar**, with "It's the" set small and stacked beside it as a typographic aside.
  - **Scattered junk-food doodles** (popsicles, donuts, milk cartons) as a sparse confetti layer. Each is a tiny outlined icon, which keeps the collage readable.
  - **Weird-cute characters:** cyclops eyes, body hair, tongues out. Imperfection makes them lovable rather than corporate.

### 3. Captain Goosebumps
- **URL:** https://goosebumps.epic.net/
- **Studio:** EPIC Agency (Belgium): https://www.epic.net/en/
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 19 Apr 2025 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/captain-goosebumps)
- **What it is:** a browser game: "This goose may not be able to fly, but it sure can slide! Collect all the orbs and bring color back to the world", made in support of the charity Live in Color.
- **What to steal:**
  - **Paper-cut layered landscape:** stacked rounded hills, each with a **grain-noise gradient** (a soft light → dark ramp plus a stipple texture). This is the "Headspace-meets-Monument-Valley" warmth.
  - **Colour as reward:** the game mechanic literally restores colour. It is a great narrative device for a playful brand ("you make the world brighter").
  - **Stylised geometric trees**, built as lollipops: circle crowns on striped trunks.

### 4. Meow Mart (Mailchimp Presents)
- **URL:** https://meowmart.mailchimp.com/
- **Creator:** Mailchimp in-house (brand by Collins, see the creators section)
- **Award:** Awwwards Honorable Mention, 14 Dec 2020 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/meow-mart)
- **What it is:** "the perils of running a small business told through the eyes of a cat in a corner store fixing a vending machine. Dodge fiery snacks. Earn coins. Buy nice hats."
- **What to steal:**
  - **A stacked-offset wordmark:** coral letters with a 1.5 px black outline, duplicated behind as a cream "ghost" shifted about 8 px down and right. It is the cheapest sticker effect in CSS (`text-shadow` or a duplicated layer with `-webkit-text-stroke`).
  - **Drippy, melting details** (the collar and tail stripes) give a hand-made, slightly off-kilter quality, matching the Collins brand's "odd personal expression" rule.
  - **Brand story told as a mini-game** instead of a features list. It is a gamified explanation of the product's value.

### 5. Ink Games
- **URL:** https://inkgames.com/games/
- **Studio:** ToyFight (Manchester): https://toyfight.co
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 14 Oct 2025 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/ink-games)
- **What to steal:**
  - **Mascot-as-logo:** a mischievous white octopus silhouette with angry brows. A single-colour character is readable at favicon size.
  - The type system is a deliberate tension: **Ruder Plakat** (a heavy poster grotesk) for display against **PP Neue Montreal / Neue Montreal Mono** for UI. Playful does not have to mean round type everywhere.

### 6. ToyFight (studio site)
- **URL:** https://toyfight.co
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 9 Nov 2016 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/toyfight)
- **What to steal:**
  - **Founders rendered as 3D action figures** on a saturated flat-yellow seamless backdrop. It turns "about us" into a toy-box joke.
  - **A brush-script wordmark** slashed across the figures adds energy against the calm studio lighting.
  - **One saturated background colour, no gradients:** the "toy product shot" convention.

### 7. Fruitful
- **URL:** https://www.fruitful.com/
- **Credited to:** David Kirschberg (on Awwwards)
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 1 Jun 2024 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/fruitful)
- **What it is:** a friendly fintech ("Stop worrying about your finances. That's our job.").
- **What to steal:**
  - **Painterly pastel clouds** (soft brush edges, aqua sky, butter-yellow highlights) take the anxiety out of money. Playful does not have to be loud; this is the **calm end** of the style.
  - **Figtree** (rounded geometric sans, free on Google Fonts) for UI and **Source Serif 4** for warmth in editorial moments.

### 8. Headspace
- **URL:** https://www.headspace.com
- **Creator:** Headspace in-house brand and illustration team
- **Award:** Webby People's Voice, Apps: Health & Fitness, 2018 ? (gallery entry https://winners.webbyawards.com/2018/apps-and-software/general-mobile-ott-apps/health-fitness/43937/headspace appeared in search results, but the page is JS-rendered and I could not read the award tier)
- **What to steal:**
  - **Blob characters with tiny dot eyes and no mouths or limbs** (a minimal baby-schema face) make meditation feel non-judgemental.
  - **Orange as the signature hue** with warm pastel supports, and generous whitespace. The illustration stays sparse so the product feels calm.
  - **A custom rounded sans** ("Headspace Apercu", a custom cut of Apercu) for all UI.

### 9. Duolingo (brand system)
- **URL:** https://www.duolingo.com
- **Studio:** Johnson Banks (2019 rebrand): https://www.johnsonbanks.co.uk/templates/work/duolingo. Typeface **Feather Bold** by Fontsmith (Krista Radoeva, creative direction Phil Garnham), per Creative Review and Monotype.
- **Award:** Webby, Best Viral PR Campaign, "The Death of Duo", 2026 ? (gallery URL in search results only)
- **What to steal:**
  - **Type derived from the mascot:** Feather Bold's flicks echo Duo's feathers. Derive one quirk in your display face from your mascot's anatomy.
  - **The mascot does everything** (errors, streaks, push notifications, social). The character *is* the brand voice.
  - **Chunky 3D buttons:** a darker bottom "lip" on each button (≈4 px) that collapses on press. Every tap is physically satisfying.

### 10. Chocapic Nutri-Game (cross-listed with EW-2)
- **URL:** https://www.chocapic-nutri-game.fr (now HTTP 410, gone)
- **Studio:** Merci-Michel (gamification and advergame studio): https://www.merci-michel.com, with McCann
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 31 Oct 2020 ✔
