# References: EW-2 Retro-Nostalgic

**Legend:** ✔ verified means I saw the award on the award body's own page (the page title and the award/date line on `awwwards.com/sites/<slug>`, fetched 2026-11-03). ? unverified means I found it only in press or search results.

**Sub-eras covered:**
- **OS** = old-OS / early-web pastiche
- **PIX** = pixel / 8-bit
- **70s** = 70s warm and soft-serif / retro-futurism
- **Y2K** = Y2K chrome / glitch
- **PRINT** = risograph, print and hand-inked

## Awarded sites

### 1. Poolside FM (now Poolsuite) · OS
- **URL:** https://poolside.fm → redirects to https://poolsuite.net
- **Creators:** Marty Bell (founder; Awwwards user "mrty"), Niek Dekker (design) and Lewis King (development), per It's Nice That (2019)
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 12 Aug 2019 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/poolside-fm)
- **What to steal:**
  - **A whole site as a fake desktop OS:** a menu bar, draggable overlapping windows with striped title bars, desktop icons labelled in a pixel font, and a marbled-blue wallpaper. Niek Dekker's brief: "as if a super fresh OS was just released, but in 1999" (It's Nice That). It is *a new old thing*, not a copy of Mac OS 9.
  - **Bitmap-style fonts:** ChiKareGo2 (a Chicago-like pixel face), "Pixelated Times New Roman" and Pixolde, as read from the live CSS.
  - **Brand-specific UI jokes:** martini glasses replace the close buttons, and there is an ASCII-art boot screen and a guestbook window. Reskin the *metaphors*, not just the pixels.
  - **VHS video loops** in windows (grain, tracking wobble) provide motion without WebGL.

### 2. basement chronicles · PIX / Y2K
- **URL:** https://chronicles.basement.studio/
- **Studio:** basement.studio: https://basement.studio
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 8 Nov 2023 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/basement-chronicles)
- **What it is:** "A pixelated throwback to classic point-and-click game on your browser."
- **What to steal:**
  - **A chiselled, bevelled chrome wordmark** (a 90s game box-art feel) with a **neon-red handwritten script** slashed over it. The clash of two eras is the hook.
  - **Ordered-dither / halftone background** (a purple vortex) reads as low-colour-depth 90s graphics.
  - **Point-and-click adventure as the navigation metaphor.** Typeface: FFFlauta (from the CSS).

### 3. Retronova · 70s retro-futurism
- **URL:** https://www.retronovaworld.com (now redirects to a Webflow 404 page)
- **Creator:** Nicola Romei: https://www.nicolaromei.com
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 8 Sep 2024 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/retronova)
- **What to steal:**
  - The **70s sci-fi paperback palette:** rust, ochre, umber and dusty olive, with heavy **film grain** over everything.
  - **Tall condensed cream display caps** (an industrial-grotesk feel, tight leading) against **monospace `//` captions**, so the retro feels engineered rather than kitsch.
  - **Matte-painting imagery** (saucer architecture in a canyon) instead of chrome: retro-futurism from the 1970s concept-art tradition.

### 4. VINYLCUTS (HBO "Vinyl") · 70s
- **URL:** (campaign microsite, offline)
- **Creator:** credited to "HBO-VINYLCUTS" on Awwwards
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 16 Aug 2016 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/vinylcuts)
- **What it is:** "inspired by HBO's VINYL and New York City circa 1973".
- **What to steal:**
  - **Disco light-beam diagonals** (magenta → orange → violet bands) over a starfield.
  - **Chromatic-aberration / RGB-split** on a geometric display wordmark.
  - **CRT scanline texture.** It makes a 1973 brief feel cinematic, not costume-party.

### 5. Chocapic Nutri-Game · PIX
- **URL:** https://www.chocapic-nutri-game.fr (now HTTP 410)
- **Studio:** Merci-Michel: https://www.merci-michel.com (with McCann)
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 31 Oct 2020 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/chocapic-nutri-game)
- **What to steal:**
  - **True pixel art on a strict grid:** 1 art pixel = N CSS pixels, with `image-rendering: pixelated`.
  - **A limited palette** (navy night, candy pink, cream tiles, glossy primary blocks) with **one-pixel highlights** for shine.
  - **Rainbow-stripe power-ups:** a modern palette inside an 8-bit grammar, so it is nostalgic without being drab.

### 6. EddyOS: Eddy Naboulet · OS / PIX
- **URL:** https://eddy-naboulet.dev/
- **Award:** Awwwards Honorable Mention, 5 Oct 2026 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/eddyos-eddy-naboulet)
- **What it is:** a portfolio "reimagined as a retro operating system, blending a CRT display, a pixel portrait, interactive windows and playful sound design."
- **What to steal:**
  - **Amber phosphor on warm black** (`#F0C27A`-ish on `#14100C`) instead of the cliché green terminal.
  - **Google pixel fonts DotGothic16 and Jacquard 12** (read from the CSS). Both are free.
  - **Sound design** as part of the OS fiction.

### 7. Rubens Cantuni portfolio · era-switcher
- **URL:** https://www.rubens.design/
- **Award:** Awwwards Honorable Mention, 10 Oct 2026 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/rubens-cantuni-1)
- **What it is:** "Five computing eras, one codebase. A timeline slider transforms every pixel (layout, typography, colors, effects) from 1992 DOS to 2077 cyberpunk."
- **What to steal:**
  - **Nostalgia as a control:** the user chooses the era. Implement it with CSS-variable theme sets (one per era).
  - Fonts include **VT323** and **Share Tech Mono** for the DOS-era skins (from the CSS).

### 8. Cozy Eating Village · PIX
- **URL:** https://www.cozyeating.app/
- **Creator:** Brooke Beswick
- **Award:** Awwwards Honorable Mention, 30 Aug 2026 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cozy-eating-village)
- **What to steal:**
  - **Isometric "cosy game" pixel art** in soft pastel greens and wood browns. Pixel does not have to mean neon.
  - **UI panels styled as wooden signboards with pixel icons,** set in the **Silkscreen** font (free, Google Fonts).

### 9. BEIGE FORCE!! · PIX / arcade
- **URL:** (linked from Awwwards)
- **Credit:** Andrew Alford Creative (per the site itself); submitted by "brandondurham"
- **Award:** Awwwards Honorable Mention, 4 Sep 2026 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/beige-force)
- **What to steal:**
  - **A Space-Invaders-style satire** with a point of view ("Beige deserves this.").
  - **A Game-Boy-green bezel frame** around a dithered pink-and-blue 3D pixel landscape.
  - **Pixel glyph tiles in a grid, labelled in monospace.**

### 10. Neutomni · retro UI
- **URL:** https://neutomni.com
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 13 Oct 2026 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/neutomni)
- **What it is:** "A bold, editorial portfolio… blending striking typography with playful retro UI."
- **What to steal:** contemporary editorial type mixed with **retro OS UI widgets** (with a Fira Code monospace in the stack). Retro is used as *seasoning*, not as the whole meal.

### 11. Luaka Bop · 70s / PRINT
- **URL:** https://www.luakabop.com/
- **Studio:** Alright Studio (New York): https://alright.studio
- **Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day, 10 Sep 2022 ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/luaka-bop)
- **What to steal:**
  - **Hand-inked psychedelic repeat patterns** (eye-in-vase motifs) as wallpaper.
  - **Helvetica Rounded Bold Condensed / Black** for display (from the CSS). Rounded condensed grotesk is a quintessential 70s record-label voice.

## Creators and studios who specialise in this style

| Creator / studio | Portfolio | Signature moves |
|---|---|---|
| **Poolsuite** (Marty Bell, Niek Dekker, Lewis King) | https://poolsuite.net | Invented-vintage OS: new metaphors (martini close buttons), bitmap fonts, VHS loops, an "escape" mood. |
| **basement.studio** (Buenos Aires) | https://basement.studio | Pixel and point-and-click storytelling, chrome bevel type, dithered textures, a self-deprecating voice. |
| **Nicola Romei** | https://www.nicolaromei.com | Cinematic 70s retro-futurism: grain, condensed caps, monospace captions, matte-painting scenes. |
| **Merci-Michel** (Paris) | https://www.merci-michel.com | Branded 8-bit and arcade advergames with strict pixel grids and playful palettes. |
| **Alright Studio** (NY) | https://alright.studio | Music and culture sites with print-era typography and hand-made pattern systems (Luaka Bop). |
| **Rubens Cantuni** | https://www.rubens.design | An era-switch theming system across computing history (DOS → 2077). |
| **CARI: Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute** | https://cari.institute | Not a studio but the reference archive that named and catalogued Y2K sub-aesthetics (Frutiger Aero, Y2K Futurism, McBling, etc.). Use it to pick an exact sub-era instead of a vague "Y2K". |
