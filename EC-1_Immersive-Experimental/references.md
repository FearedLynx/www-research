# EC-1 Immersive / Experimental: references

**How awards were verified (checked 2026-11-03).**
- **SOTY badges** come from the Awwwards "Sites of the Year" gallery (`https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_year/`). That page lists each site with a "SOTY · Site Of The Year <year>" badge and a "Developer Award" badge. The gallery does **not** separate the overall winner from other SOTY-badged entries in a year (several sites carry the badge for the same year). So "SOTY badge ✔" means *the badge appears on the Awwwards gallery*. "Winner ✔" is used only where a second source names the site as *the* winner.
- **SOTD dates** come from each site's Awwwards page (`https://www.awwwards.com/sites/<slug>/`). Tech tags quoted are Awwwards' own tags on that page.
- **✔ verified** means I saw it on the award page or the studio's own site. **? unverified** means I believe it but did not see it on a primary page.
- FWA and CSSDA pages render client-side and could not be read, so every FWA/CSSDA mention below is **? unverified**.

## A. Sites (17)

### 1. Igloo Inc
- **URL:** https://www.igloo.inc/
- **Studio:** abeto, https://abeto.co/
- **Awards:** Awwwards **SOTY 2024 badge plus Developer Award ✔** (SOTY gallery). **Winner, Site of the Year 2024** is reported by search summaries of the Awwwards Annual Awards 2024 page and a University of Waterloo alumni article ("alumni accepts award on behalf of Igloo Inc"), but I did not read the annual page directly (?). SOTD 23 Jul 2024, score 7.92 ✔. Awwwards tags: Animation, Infinite Scroll, Transitions, 3D ✔.
- **What to steal:**
  - The brand concept (an igloo, so ice) is rendered as a *material*: refractive, crystalline ice geometry rather than a logo on a plane.
  - An **infinite vertical scroll** that loops the scene, so scrolling never "ends".
  - A restrained two-colour UI (Awwwards palette: `#b6bac5`, `#383e4e`) with all colour and light coming from the rendering.
  - Section changes are camera moves inside one scene, not page cuts.

### 2. Messenger
- **URL:** https://messenger.abeto.co/
- **Studio:** abeto, https://abeto.co/
- **Awards:** Awwwards **SOTY 2025 badge plus Developer Award ✔**. SOTD 10 Nov 2025 ✔. Tags: Games & Entertainment, Storytelling, 3D, WebGL, WebSockets ✔.
- **What to steal:**
  - A complete **multiplayer browser game** as a "website". The tagline, "It's a small planet, but someone's gotta make the deliveries.", is the whole brief in one line.
  - A tiny spherical world: the camera orbits a planet, so the horizon curves and everything feels toy-like (the warm EC-1 variant).
  - WebSockets presence: you see other visitors, so the site feels alive.

### 3. Lando Norris
- **URL:** https://landonorris.com/
- **Studio:** OFF+BRAND., https://itsoffbrand.com/
- **Awards:** Awwwards **SOTY 2025 badge plus Developer Award ✔**. SOTD 17 Nov 2025 ✔. Tags: WebGL, GSAP, Webflow, 3D, Gestures/Interaction ✔. Lenis and ScrollTrigger references found in the page HTML ✔.
- **What to steal:**
  - It proves an immersive build can run on **Webflow plus custom WebGL/GSAP**: a CMS-driven site with award-level motion.
  - The athlete's helmet palette (neon accent) is used as the single accent colour.
  - Modular layout separating "on track" and "off track" content. Spectacle in the hero, plain structure for news and merch (the MAYA principle in practice).
