Manifesto

Most websites are documents. Ours are instruments. They respond to your hand, your scroll and the hour you visit. We treat the browser as a stage, the GPU as a lighting rig, and every frame as a frame we owe you.

How a frame gets made

Four steps / scroll sideways

01

Listen

Two weeks of workshops before a single vertex. We write the story as a camera path: where you start, what you notice, where you land.

OutputCamera script
02

Sketch in code

Every idea is a live prototype by Friday. Shaders get judged in the browser, on a mid-range Android, not in a slide.

Prototypes / project~40
03

Budget the frame

16.6 ms is the brief. We bake light, compress geometry with Draco and KTX2, and drop quality tiers the moment the GPU struggles.

Target60 fps / LCP < 2.5 s
04

Ship with a quiet mode

Every site ships a reduced-motion version that is designed, not stripped. Same story, still frames, full content.
