## B. Canon of web brutalism (no award claimed)

| Site | URL | Creator | What to steal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brutalist Websites** | https://brutalistwebsites.com | Pascal Deville, founded 2014 (creative director at Freundliche Grüsse, Zürich, per Boing Boing 2016 / Fast Company 2016 coverage) | The archive itself is brutalist: monospace on `#eee`, 400×250 screenshot boxes floated left, creator interviews. Best single source of examples. |
| **Brutalist Web Design guidelines** | https://brutalist-web.design | David Bryant Copeland | Seven functional rules (readable everywhere, only links/buttons clickable, underlined links, back button works, scrolling, decoration only when needed, performance as a feature). |
| **Low←Tech Magazine (solar)** | https://solar.lowtechmagazine.com | Kris De Decker and team | Solar-powered server, dithered images, battery level shown on the page. Rawness with a real ethical reason. |
| **Aphex Twin store** | https://aphextwin.warp.net | Warp Records | Pixel font everywhere, tracklists as black-highlighted underlined rows with BPMs in brackets, giant ghost text in the background, blurred monochrome photo. The music-label reference. |
| **Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam** | https://www.stedelijk.nl | Stedelijk Museum | Full-width rows of giant grotesk type separated by thick black rules, artworks inserted between words, outlined type for the collection, text cut off at the right edge. |
| **Gerrit Rietveld Academie** | https://rietveldacademie.nl | Rietveld Academie | Front page curated weekly by a different student; default underlined links, large plain grotesk paragraph, gradient fade into a raw photo. |
| **MCA Chicago** and **Bloomberg "Government-sponsored cyber militia cookbook" (2018)** | https://mcachicago.org · bloomberg.com feature | Institutions | Both are on brutalistwebsites.com; screenshots in `images/`. |
| **MSCHF** | https://mschf.com | MSCHF, New York | Art collective whose drops and site are deliberately abrasive; logo `!!!` in red on black. |
| **Experimental Jetset archive** | https://www.jetset.nl | Experimental Jetset, Amsterdam | Near-raw HTML project archive from Dutch modernists. |
| **Yale School of Art** | https://art.yale.edu | Yale School of Art | Long cited as the classic example of a wiki-editable, ever-changing brutalist school site (current version not re-inspected). |

## D. Style-specific reading

- Ganci, A. & Ribeiro, B. (2017). On Web Brutalism and Contemporary Web Design. *Dialectic*, 1(1). doi:10.3998/dialectic.14932326.0001.107 (Crossref-confirmed; summary via AIGA Design Educators listing)
- Suárez-Carballo, F. (2019). The visual language of brutalist web design. *Doxa Comunicación*, 28, 111–131. (Found via DOAJ / CEU repository; full text not read.)
- Rebelo, J. N., Rebelo, S. M. & Rebelo, A. (2021). Algorithmic Experiments in the Design of Brutalist Web Pages. *ARTECH 2021: 10th International Conference on Digital and Interactive Arts.* ACM. doi:10.1145/3483529.3483746 (Crossref-confirmed)
- Banham, R. (1966). *The New Brutalism: Ethic or Aesthetic?* Architectural Press. Origin of the term's "truth to materials" meaning.
- Hekkert, P., Snelders, D. & van Wieringen, P. C. W. (2003). 'Most advanced, yet acceptable': Typicality and novelty as joint predictors of aesthetic preference in industrial design. *British Journal of Psychology*, 94(1). doi:10.1348/000712603762842147
- Tuch, A. N. et al. (2012). The role of visual complexity and prototypicality regarding first impression of websites. *IJHCS*, 70(11). doi:10.1016/j.ijhcs.2012.06.003
- Berlyne, D. E. (1971). *Aesthetics and Psychobiology.* Appleton-Century-Crofts.
- Poynor, R. (2003). *No More Rules: Graphic Design and Postmodernism.* Laurence King. Background on anti-design in print (Carson, Emigre, Cranbrook).
- Journalism: Boing Boing, "The return to a simpler, uglier web" (31 May 2016); Fast Company, "The Internet's 10 Ugliest Websites" (2016); Creative Bloq, "Are brutalist sites the web's punk rock moment?".
- Lavie & Tractinsky (2004) and other general papers: see `00_Research/academic-findings.md`.
