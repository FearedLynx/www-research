# CC-3 Tech Product: references

Verification legend: **✔ verified** = award label and date seen on the award's own page (awwwards.com/sites/…), checked 3 Nov 2026. **? unverified** = believed but not confirmed. **industry-reference** = highly influential, no formal award found. Many of the most-copied SaaS sites (Linear, Raycast, Resend, Cursor) have **no** formal web award; their influence comes from the industry, not from juries. "Credits" are the Awwwards profiles attached to the entry.

---

## A. Reference sites

### 1. Next.js Conf 2024
- **URL:** https://nextjs.org/conf
- **Creator:** basement.studio (https://basement.studio) with the Vercel design team (Awwwards credits: basementstudio, 0ca0a, evilrabb.it, genny-dee)
- **Award:** Awwwards **Site of the Day, 16 Sep 2024** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/next-js-conf-1). The 2022 edition: **Honorable Mention, 12 Sep 2022** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/next-js-conf)
- **What to steal:** monochrome black-and-white with one 3D/WebGL centrepiece; Geist type at large sizes with tight tracking; a ticket-generation mechanic (personalised OG ticket) as a viral loop; a visible-grid layout with crosshair corners.

### 2. Vercel Ship
- **URL:** https://vercel.com/ship
- **Creator:** basement.studio + Vercel (credits: basementstudio, 0ca0a, evilrabb.it, genny-dee)
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 5 Mar 2024** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/vercel-ship)
- **What to steal:** Vercel's system at event scale. Pure black, the backlit triangle (see `images/vercel_og.png`: a black prism with a white rim glow and grain), hairline grids, mono metadata, and restraint: a single light source per screen.

### 3. Tailscale
- **URL:** https://tailscale.com
- **Creator:** Together (London), https://together.agency, with Will Beeching
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 18 Mar 2024** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/tailscale)
- **What to steal:** a *light-mode* devtool that still reads technical. Network diagrams drawn as the art direction (nodes and edges are the product's metaphor), warm off-white backgrounds, friendly copy. Proof that CC-3 does not have to be dark.

### 4. Stytch: auth and fraud prevention APIs
- **URL:** https://stytch.com
- **Creator:** Together, https://together.agency
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 4 Oct 2022** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/stytch)
- **What to steal:** login UI components shown as live, themeable widgets beside their code; tabbed SDK samples; security product framed with CC-2-style trust cues.

### 5. Coder: cloud dev environments
- **URL:** https://coder.com
- **Creator:** Together with Will Beeching
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 20 Nov 2025** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/coder)
- **What to steal:** enterprise devtool positioning; terminal and IDE chrome as hero imagery; enterprise logos and compliance next to developer-native visuals.

### 6. VoidZero: JavaScript toolchain (Vite, Vitest, Rolldown, Oxc)
- **URL:** https://voidzero.dev
- **Creator:** Together, https://together.agency
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 8 Mar 2026** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/voidzero)
- **What to steal:** an open-source toolchain presented as a coherent product family. Each tool gets a distinct light/colour inside one dark system; benchmark numbers used as hero proof.

### 7. Evervault: encryption infrastructure
- **URL:** https://evervault.com
- **Creator:** in-house, Nev Flynn credited
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 17 Feb 2023** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/evervault)
- **What to steal:** the domain metaphor as art. Encrypted-text "scramble" effects and character-grid textures make "encryption" visible; hover reveals plaintext and ciphertext.

### 8. Trunk: CI reliability and merge queue
- **URL:** https://trunk.io
- **Creator:** basement.studio
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 5 Feb 2024** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/trunk)
- **What to steal:** a *dev* tool with basement's attitude: bolder colour, 3D and playful motion while keeping product screenshots central.

### 9. Cal.com: open-source scheduling
- **URL:** https://cal.com
- **Creator:** basement.studio with Peer Richelsen and Jose Rago (credited)
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 7 Oct 2022** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cal-com)
- **What to steal:** the booking widget embedded live in the hero (try the product without signing up); light-mode SaaS with black type.

### 10. Attio: CRM
- **URL:** https://attio.com
- **Creator:** Ana Moreno credited on Awwwards
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 6 Apr 2021** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/attio)
- **What to steal:** data-table UI as beautiful imagery; precise light theme with crisp 1px borders.

### 11. Linear
- **URL:** https://linear.app
- **Creator:** in-house; co-founder and CEO Karri Saarinen (ex-Airbnb, Coinbase design) leads brand; Emil Kowalski (design engineer at Linear, https://emilkowal.ski)
- **Award:** **industry-reference (no formal award found)**; the slugs awwwards.com/sites/linear and /linear-app return 404.
- **What to steal:** the original of the genre (Ginn 2023: "the Linear effect"). Product UI rendered at the fold with perspective and soft glow; white-to-grey gradient headings; a near-black `#08090A`-like surface; keyboard-shortcut storytelling; the "Linear Method" essays as brand philosophy. Their 2024 UI redesign posts explain how they cut visual noise.

### 12. Vercel (main site) and Geist
- **URL:** https://vercel.com · https://vercel.com/geist
- **Creator:** Vercel design team (design engineer Rauno Freiberg, https://rauno.me) · Geist typeface by Vercel with basement.studio, designers Andrés Briganti and Mateo Zaragoza
- **Award:** main site **industry-reference** (event sites above are awarded ✔)
- **What to steal:** visible grid lines with "+" intersections, a black/white palette with semantic colours only, Geist Sans + Geist Mono, dense but legible docs-grade typography, and an open design system (Geist) as a brand asset.

### 13. Raycast
- **URL:** https://raycast.com
- **Creator:** in-house design team (founders ex-Apple per interviews)
- **Award:** **industry-reference (no formal award found)**
- **What to steal:** red light shafts on black (see `images/raycast_og.png`), the command palette itself as the hero with a lit red top edge, 3D keycaps, white-to-grey gradient headline "Your shortcut to everything.", and an extension store that turns community into content.

### 14. Resend
- **URL:** https://resend.com
- **Creator:** in-house (founder Zeno Rocha; brand designers incl. Zá Scalon and Manoel do Amaral, per Resend's blog)
- **Award:** **industry-reference (no formal award found)**
- **What to steal:** the cleanest escape from the Inter monoculture: a high-contrast **serif** headline with a silver gradient ("Email for Developers"), a code sample with language tabs as hero imagery, and dark 3D renders. Developer focus without neon.

### Also worth a look
- **Supabase** (https://supabase.com, in-house, industry-reference): brand green `#3ECF8E` on dark, launch-week microsites, code-first hero.
- **Cursor** (https://cursor.com, industry-reference): wide-caps wordmark on warm black (`images/cursor_og.png`), product video of the editor as hero.
- **Warp, Clerk, PlanetScale, Railway** (industry-reference): OG images in `images/` for comparison of how each signals "developer".
- **Reflect** (notes app): Awwwards Honorable Mention 11 May 2023 ✔. Glowing gradient orbs on dark done well.
- **Stripe Sessions 2023**: Awwwards Honorable Mention 4 May 2023 ✔. Event-scale CC-3 from a CC-2 company.
- **Daylight Computer** (basement.studio): Awwwards SOTD 18 Jun 2024 ✔. Hardware cousin of the style.
