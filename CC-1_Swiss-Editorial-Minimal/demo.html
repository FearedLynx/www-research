A — 02

Three rules we do not break

01

The grid comes first.

Every plan starts as seven equal bays. Walls, windows and furniture find their place inside it, so the building stays legible as it changes over decades.

02

Nothing is decoration.

If an element has no structural, climatic or social job, we remove it. What remains is allowed to be beautiful.

03

Build for a hundred years.

Brick, timber, lime render. Materials that age honestly, repair simply and can be taken apart again.
