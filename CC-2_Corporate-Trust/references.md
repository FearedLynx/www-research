# CC-2 Corporate Trust: references

Verification legend: **✔ verified** = I saw the award label and date on the award's own page (awwwards.com/sites/…, winners.webbyawards.com) or in a dated press report quoting the winner. **? unverified** = believed but not confirmed. **industry-reference** = influential site with no formal award found.

## A. Reference sites

### 1. Clear Street: capital-markets infrastructure
- **URL:** https://www.clearstreet.io/
- **Creator:** Bürocratik (Büro), Coimbra/Porto, Portugal: https://burocratik.com
- **Award:** Awwwards **Site of the Day, 5 Dec 2024** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/clear-street)
- **What to steal:**
  - A saturated electric-blue field with white product panels: institutional, but unmistakable at thumbnail size (see `images/clearstreet_og.jpg`).
  - Hero line "Speed, Transparency and Scale for Sophisticated Investors™". The trademark symbol on a tagline signals institution-level formality.
  - A metrics band with approximate, unit-labelled numbers ("~706m shares / day", "~$28.4bn notional / day", "~3,800 clients"). The tilde reads as honesty.
  - Regulatory lines (FINRA, SIPC, CFTC, NFA) treated as part of the page, not hidden.
  - A coverage map listing exchanges (ASX, EUREX, JPX) as proof of reach.

### 2. NexBank: institutional and commercial bank
- **URL:** https://www.nexbank.com
- **Creator:** Rejouice (San Diego / Paris), https://www.rejouice.com, with Oui Will (credited on Awwwards)
- **Award:** Awwwards **Site of the Day, 29 Jan 2021** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/nexbank)
- **What to steal:** a traditional bank told with editorial pacing: large statement typography, slow reveal transitions and segment-specific paths (institutional / commercial / mortgage) as the primary navigation. Proves a 100-year-old category can win SOTD without abandoning conservative structure.

### 3. Aston Currency Management: FX and payments
- **URL:** https://astoncm.com/
- **Creator:** Outpost (Awwwards profile /Outpost/), with Jesper Landberg, Jesper Westlund and Stephen Hannon credited
- **Award:** Awwwards **Site of the Day, 26 Jan 2022** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/aston-currency-management)
- **What to steal:** smooth-scroll storytelling (Jesper Landberg is known for WebGL and smooth-scroll craft) applied to a sober FX brand. Motion is used for *continuity between sections*, not spectacle. Tagline-led ("Currency without limits").

### 4. Jeton: e-wallet / payments
- **URL:** https://jeton.com
- **Creator:** Bürocratik, https://burocratik.com
- **Award:** Awwwards **Site of the Day, 27 Jan 2025** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/jeton)
- **What to steal:** how a consumer-facing payments brand can be bold (big colour fields, oversized type) and still feel regulated. Compare with Clear Street (same studio, B2B) to see one studio flex the style between audiences.

### 5. Runway: FP&A software for finance teams
- **URL:** https://runway.com
- **Creator:** Reform Collective, https://reformcollective.com. Credits: Eric Hoffman (CEO & CD), Brandon Zacharias (ACD), David Campbell (lead dev), Dallen Hoyal, Evan Vollick-Offer
- **Award:** **Webby Award Winner 2024**, Websites, Financial Services & Banking ✔ (https://winners.webbyawards.com/2024/websites-and-mobile-sites/general-desktop-mobile-sites/financial-servicesbanking/275371/runway)
- **What to steal:** humane voice in a spreadsheet category (their five-word speech: "Finance People Are People Too"); warm, crafted art direction around a model/graph product; a playful brand that still lets the product UI carry the proof.

### 6. Rogo: AI analyst for investment banking
- **URL:** https://rogo.com (Awwwards lists rogodata.com)
- **Creator:** Together (London), https://together.agency, with Will Beeching credited
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 28 Mar 2024** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/rogo)
- **What to steal:** selling AI to bankers. Restrained palette, serious typography and analyst-style output (tables, sourced answers) instead of sparkle-emoji AI tropes. Shows how Corporate Trust absorbs AI without looking like a chatbot landing page.

### 7. Wealthsimple: consumer investing
- **URL:** https://www.wealthsimple.com
- **Creator:** in-house creative team with Doberman (design), ManvsMachine (animation) and Varvet (development), per BetaKit's report
- **Awards:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 10 Nov 2015** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/wealthsimple); **Webby Award, Financial Services/Banking, 2016 and 2017** ✔ (reported by BetaKit, https://betakit.com/?p=96867: "second consecutive year" in 2017)
- **What to steal:** the template for "warm trust". Serif display, generous whitespace, editorial photography of real people, plain-English money copy. It moved investing from Wall Street navy to lifestyle-magazine calm.

### 8. Unit: banking-as-a-service infrastructure
- **URL:** https://unit.co
- **Creator:** Together, https://together.agency
- **Award:** Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 24 Sep 2021** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/unit)
- **What to steal:** explaining invisible infrastructure (accounts, cards, compliance APIs) through clean diagrams and modular product panels. Good model for "we are the plumbing behind your bank".

### 9. Stripe: payments infrastructure
- **URL:** https://stripe.com
- **Creator:** Stripe in-house brand and design teams (head of design Katie Dill has spoken publicly about the homepage evolution); typeface Söhne by Klim Type Foundry, https://klim.co.nz/retail-fonts/soehne/
- **Award:** Awwwards *Nominee* 2016 only (not an award) ✔. Stripe Sessions 2023 promo site: Awwwards **Honorable Mention, 4 May 2023** ✔ (https://www.awwwards.com/sites/stripe-sessions-2023). Main site: **industry-reference (no formal award found)**.
- **What to steal:** the animated WebGL mesh gradient (rendered with a tiny in-house WebGL helper; reverse-engineered by Kevin Hufnagl, see https://www.bram.us/2021/10/13/how-to-create-the-stripe-website-gradient-effect); skewed section edges; code samples beside marketing copy; the navy `#0A2540` + blurple `#635BFF` pairing; dense, information-rich mega-menus that signal breadth. Daryl Ginn (2023) calls Stripe "the old Linear": the site the whole category copied.

### 10. Mercury: banking for startups
- **URL:** https://mercury.com
- **Creator:** Mercury in-house Brand Studio (their job posts describe bringing back "care and craft" to finance)
- **Award:** **industry-reference (no formal award found)**. Note: awwwards.com/sites/mercury is a *different* company (an NZ energy firm).
- **What to steal:** dusk-toned blurred photographic backdrop behind a crisp dark dashboard (see `images/mercury_og.jpg`); superscript cents in balances; a real product UI with plausible company names as the hero; editorial "Mercury Raise / Meridian" content programmes as authority builders.

### 11. Ramp: corporate cards and spend management
- **URL:** https://ramp.com
- **Creator:** in-house brand team; Webflow published a case study on their six-week site rebuild (https://www.casestudies.com/company/webflow-inc/case-study/how-ramp-redesigned-their-website-in-just-six-weeks-with-webflow)
- **Award:** **industry-reference (no formal award found)**
- **What to steal:** one owned colour (yellow) in a sea of fintech blue; savings numbers as the headline ("save X%" with methodology); a customer-quote-with-dollar-figure pattern.

### 12. Increase and Column: developer-first banks
- **URLs:** https://increase.com · https://column.com
- **Creator:** in-house
- **Award:** **industry-reference (no formal award)**
- **What to steal:** extreme restraint. Near-documentation layouts, monospaced details, no illustrations, and a bank charter stated plainly. This is the "nothing to hide" end of the spectrum and a useful antidote to over-designed fintech.
