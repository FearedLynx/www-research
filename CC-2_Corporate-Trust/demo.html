The platform

Four entries on one ledger.

No dashboards for their own sake. Corvane does four jobs a controller would otherwise do by hand, and logs every action with a timestamp your auditor can test.

01

Consolidate

Connect every bank, card and brokerage account by API or SFTP. One balance, refreshed every 15 minutes, with no screen-scraping.

38 banksdirect connections
02

Sweep

Set a floor for each operating account. Cash above it moves to a segregated T-bill ladder overnight and comes back the same day you need it.

T+0liquidity on redemptions
03

Reconcile

Every inflow and outflow is matched to your ERP by 7 a.m. Exceptions go to a named person, not a shared inbox.
