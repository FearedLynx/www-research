Soil to slice

Four steps, no shortcuts, about eleven months.

Regenerative farming means the field gets better every year we use it. Our job is to get out of the way of the soil, the grain and the starter.

October

Sow

Direct-drilled into last year's stubble with clover between the rows. No plough, no bare soil, no synthetic nitrogen.

Tuesdays

Mill

Whole grain, one slow pass on granite. The germ and bran stay in, so the flour tastes of the field and keeps for about six weeks.

36 hours

Ferment

Our starter, Mabel, is eleven years old. She does most of the work while we sleep.

Wednesdays

Bake

Wood-fired at 5am, on the bench by 9. When the 140 loaves are gone, the bakery closes and we go back to the field.
